FTTH · distributed split design

FTTH Design Studio

Places drop terminals and splitters, draws MST and distribution-area boundaries, routes and sizes every cable along real streets, adds handholes and splice points, checks the optical loss to each home and prices the build. Upload your address points and the map zooms straight to them; add a boundary, OLT location and road centerlines for a fuller design. Apartment complexes are flagged as MDUs and left out. Double-click the map to move the OLT.

Design your area

Upload address points and the map zooms to them. A boundary, OLT location and road centerlines (free from most county GIS sites) make the design more accurate. Accepted: shapefile (.zip, or .shp + .dbf + .prj selected together), GeoJSON, KML or KMZ, GPX, Excel or OpenDocument sheets, and CSV or tab-separated text with latitude/longitude (or X/Y) columns or a WKT column. For GeoPackage, DXF or DWG, export to one of these first.

    Flagged where 3 or more addresses share one spot, or a units field shows 3 or more. Unit counts assume 2 floors and 90 m² per unit; confirm on site.
